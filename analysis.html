Analysis

Three analysis tools. The first two take your own MetaPhlAn profile; the third runs across projects in the database.

Microbiome composition

Generate taxonomic composition bar plots and tables for selected samples. [Planned: not yet available.]

Sample similarity search

Find the database samples most similar to a query profile, by Bray–Curtis distance on species abundances. [Planned.]

Cross-study analysis

Compare shared and unique taxa across cohorts, diseases and sample sites. [Planned.]

Upload a MetaPhlAn table

Upload a merged MetaPhlAn taxonomy table (merge_metaphlan_tables.py output: one row per clade, one column per sample). The table is checked and previewed in your browser; nothing is sent to a server yet.

Do not upload personal data. Profiles must be pseudonymised and handled under an approved ethics permit and GDPR.

Expected format 
clade_name	S001	S002
k__Bacteria|p__Firmicutes|c__Bacilli|o__Lactobacillales|f__Lactobacillaceae|g__Lactobacillus|s__Lactobacillus_crispatus	45.2	1.3
k__Bacteria|p__Bacteroidota|c__Bacteroidia|o__Bacteroidales|f__Prevotellaceae|g__Prevotella|s__Prevotella_bivia	0.4	12.8

Rows at species level contain |s__. Rows with |t__ (strain) are ignored in the species count.
